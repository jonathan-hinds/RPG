# Frost Wave Image Generation Prompts

The six source atlases were generated with the built-in image generation tool, using a flat `#ff00ff` chroma key. The project copies preserve the generated sources before alpha cleanup.

## Rings, ground frost, and runes

Create a square 4-by-4 atlas of sixteen isolated, top-down, hand-painted frost motifs for a classic colorful fantasy MMORPG spell: irregular broken broad rings, thin bright rings, branching radial cracks, frozen patches with large gaps, faint circular frost runes, fractured starbursts, and irregular erosion shapes. Use white, cyan, saturated blue, and cobalt. Keep each motif centered in its cell with generous padding. Rings must be hollow and uneven. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` background with no grid, text, shadow, border, scenery, or magenta inside the motifs.

## Mist, vapor, snow, and noise

Create a square 4-by-4 atlas of sixteen isolated painterly frost sprites: low ground mist wisps, compact cold vapor puffs, curved wind/snow streak clouds, irregular haze puffs, cloudy distortion/erosion patches, and sparse snow/frost-speck clusters. Keep silhouettes varied, airy, readable, and chunky enough for chroma removal. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` background with no floor, grid, text, shadow, border, scenery, or magenta inside the sprites.

## Glow, snowflakes, sparkles, and streaks

Create a square 4-by-4 atlas of sixteen isolated frost particle sprites: one circular blue-white glow, three stylized snowflakes, three diamond sparkles, three tapered horizontal frost streaks, two compact impact flashes, two sparse frost-speck clusters, one curved wind streak, and one fractured ice burst. Use crisp exaggerated silhouettes and white/cyan/blue/cobalt values. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` background with no grid, text, shadow, border, lens flare, scenery, or magenta inside the sprites.

## Ice shards

Create a square 4-by-4 atlas of sixteen isolated hand-painted fantasy ice shards with varied wedge, plate, spike, hook, splinter, and diamond silhouettes. Use opaque painted facets, cobalt shadow planes, saturated blue midtones, cyan edges, and white highlights. Avoid realistic transparent glass. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` background with no floor, grid, text, shadow, border, scenery, or magenta inside the shards.

## Upright radial frost-cloud front

Create a production-ready 4-by-4 atlas of sixteen isolated side-profile, horizontally directional freezing-vapor blasts for upright world-space quads traveling radially away from a player. Use dense white-blue powder at the leading edge, a curling turbulent crest, swept-back wispy tails, cyan rim light, cobalt internal shadow, and crystalline flecks. Vary compact rolling clouds, tall breaking waves, split crests, low fast sheets, explosive powder kicks, and hooked curls. Do not create top-down rings, circles, ground decals, or flat fog patches. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` background with generous empty margin, no grid, text, border, or glow bleeding into the key color.

## Hero ice breakers

Create a production-ready 4-by-4 atlas of sixteen isolated large asymmetrical freeze formations with dramatic silhouettes: crown-like clusters of long faceted ice fangs, breaking glacier wedges, hooked translucent blue blades, opposing jaw-like spikes, and explosive ice-breaker ridges. Make them weighty and distinct from ordinary Frost Shock shards with cobalt cores, luminous cyan edges, frosted white fracture faces, and internal cracks. Use three-quarter side views and vary tall crowns, low wedges, split jaws, spear clusters, curved breakers, fractured ridges, and impact blooms. Use a perfectly flat `#ff00ff` background with generous empty margin and no characters, spell circles, ground decals, text, borders, or grid lines.
