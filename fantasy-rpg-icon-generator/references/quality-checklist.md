# Quality Checklist

Reject or revise the icon when any critical item fails.

## Meaning

- Can a viewer make a reasonable guess about the mechanic at 32x32?
- Is the primary symbol concrete and immediately visible?
- Does an active ability depict an action?
- Does a status icon depict the actual condition?

## Composition

- Is there one dominant focal point?
- Does the subject occupy roughly 70-90% of the canvas?
- Is the silhouette readable?
- Is the brightest contrast near the focal point?
- Are the corners subordinate rather than distracting?

## Style

- Does the icon look hand-painted rather than photographic or like a generic 3D render?
- Are broad shapes more important than tiny details?
- Are highlights concentrated and shadows simplified?
- Are colors rich without becoming muddy or indiscriminately neon?

## Materials

- Can metal, cloth, leather, wood, stone, bone, crystal, and magic be distinguished where relevant?
- Are effects supporting the subject rather than covering it?

## Cleanliness

- No text, pseudo-text, letters, numbers, signatures, or logos.
- No accidental permanent border.
- No unrelated extra object.
- No scenery or horizon line unless explicitly required.
- No malformed anatomy or impossible item construction.

## Family consistency

- Does it match the approved icons' crop, lighting intensity, saturation, brush scale, and background density?
- Is it still visually distinct from neighboring icons?
- Does the family avoid repeating the same silhouette or color arrangement too often?

## Technical

- Master is square and at least 1024x1024 when possible.
- Important content survives 256x256, 64x64, and 32x32 exports.
- No unintended transparency or edge contamination.
- File naming follows the required convention.
