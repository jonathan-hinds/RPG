# Quality Checklist

Reject or regenerate when any critical answer is no.

## Critical readability

- Is the subject identifiable at 32x32?
- Does one dominant silhouette read before surface detail?
- Does the subject occupy most of the square?
- Is the focal highlight attached to the important feature?
- Are foreground and background clearly separated?

## Style match

- Does it resemble compact painted inventory art more than cinematic concept art?
- Are shapes chunky, simplified, and graphic?
- Are materials suggested with a few marks rather than simulated realistically?
- Is the background quiet and abstract?
- Does it avoid the over-rendered negative reference?

## Detail control

- Could half the small scratches, links, rivets, particles, or cracks be removed without losing meaning? If yes, remove them.
- Is detail concentrated near one focal area?
- Are broad shapes still visible after downscaling?
- Is glow controlled rather than fogging the entire icon?

## Rarity

- Does the assigned rarity visibly affect craftsmanship and complexity?
- Does low rarity remain readable and intentionally simple?
- Does high rarity gain purposeful identity rather than random decoration?
- Is rarity conveyed by the item art even without a border?

## Content safety

- No copied game icon or exact named-item design.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, or accidental pseudo-writing.
- No metallic rim, beveled slot, rectangular outline, rarity border, or permanent UI frame unless explicitly requested for that exact image.
- No unrelated props, character, room, landscape, or pedestal.

- Does the background reach the canvas edges with only a soft painted vignette rather than a border?
