# Rarity Art Direction

Use rarity to control **visual complexity and presentation intensity**, not just border color. This is a project convention inspired by classic MMORPG inventory readability; it is not a claim that every World of Warcraft icon follows a strict rarity-to-detail rule.

## Shared rule

Every rarity must remain a compact inventory icon, not a cinematic illustration. Keep one item, a tight crop, chunky readable shapes, simplified materials, and a quiet abstract background.

## Poor

- Crude, damaged, improvised, or mundane construction.
- One dominant material and very few accents.
- Muted, dirty, or desaturated palette.
- Low ornamentation and little or no magical glow.
- Broadest shapes and least internal detail.
- Background nearly flat and unobtrusive.

## Common

- Practical, clean, ordinary adventuring gear.
- One or two readable materials.
- Simple construction with limited trim.
- Moderate local color but restrained effects.
- Minimal ornamentation.

## Uncommon

- Clearly upgraded craftsmanship.
- Stronger color identity and one memorable accent.
- Additional trim, inset material, rune, gem, or magical cue.
- Controlled glow or reflected color may appear.
- More silhouette character without becoming ornate.

## Rare

- Distinctive, intentionally designed equipment.
- Richer colors, stronger highlights, and cleaner material separation.
- Two or three purposeful ornamental features.
- Visible magical energy, enchantment, or unusual craftsmanship.
- More dramatic perspective, but still readable and compact.

## Epic

- Heroic silhouette and unmistakable visual identity.
- Bold color contrast, magical core, runes, gems, or elaborate forged forms.
- Strong controlled glow and dramatic accent lighting.
- Dense detail only near the focal area; edges remain readable.
- Avoid filling every surface with filigree.

## Legendary

- Iconic, singular, story-bearing design.
- Extremely strong silhouette and concentrated visual hierarchy.
- Signature magical phenomenon or material treatment.
- Heroic lighting and exceptional ornamentation, but still broad and readable.
- The item must look important without becoming a full scene or cinematic render.

## Rarity must not do this

- Do not make low rarity blurry, ugly, or unreadable.
- Do not make high rarity photorealistic.
- Do not add more unrelated objects as rarity increases.
- Do not use excessive particles, tiny filigree, or complex scenery.
- Do not rely on a baked colored border to communicate rarity.
